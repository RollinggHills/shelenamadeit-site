Bespoke & pricing

Can you recreate a dress I saw online?

No. Shelena only creates original designs; that's what makes a Shelena Made It piece yours alone. Tell her what you love about it (the feeling, the colour, the drama) and she'll design something entirely new.

How much does a bespoke piece cost?

Every design is original and priced individually. You're paying for design, not just sewing. As a guide: bridal from $1,800, prom and evening from $1,200, suits from $600 and children's couture from $400. Your consultation ends with an exact quote.

What happens at the consultation?

A relaxed 30-minute design conversation, in the studio or by video, about your occasion, the feeling you want and your budget. No need to bring photos of other dresses: words, colours and memories are perfect. Consultations are $75.

How do payments work?

1. Design fee. A design fee reserves Shelena's time to design and illustrate your original piece. It's non-refundable, and it counts toward your commission if you continue.

2. Production deposit. Love your design? A 50% deposit begins production and pays for your fabrics. It's non-refundable because materials are bought and work begins for you.

3. Balance. Due before your piece is delivered.

Designs remain Shelena's until they're commissioned, so a design can't be taken elsewhere to be made.

Do you do alterations?

Fittings are part of every commission, and Shelena will always care for a piece she made. She doesn't alter or remake garments from other designers or stores.

Do you make fashion illustrations?

Yes. Custom fashion illustrations are usually ready within 5 days to 2 weeks. You can order one on its own or add a keepsake illustration to your commission: request an illustration.

Timing & rush orders

I'm not in Austin. Can I still commission Shelena?

Absolutely. Your design consultation and presentation happen by video. Shelena guides your measurements remotely, a toile (practice garment) can be mailed for a video fitting on request, and your finished piece ships insured to your door. A fitting visit to Austin can also be arranged. How remote commissions work.

How long does a bespoke piece take?

Typically 2–4 months, depending on the design. For weddings, booking 6 months or more ahead leaves the most room for fittings.

Can you rush my order?

Yes. Rush service takes 1–2 months for an extra $200, and expedited shipping is available. Tell us your date when you book.

My event is in a few weeks. Is it too late?

Not always. Send a request with your date and Shelena will tell you honestly what's possible.

Shipping & returns

Where do you ship from?

All orders ship from our studio in Texas.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. International shipping is charged by destination and the weight of the garment.

How quickly will my order ship?

Ready-to-wear usually ships within one business day. Bespoke pieces ship within a day of completion by your chosen method. Need it by a specific date? Let us know before ordering.

Can I return my order?

Bespoke pieces are made to your measurements, so they can't be returned, but we'll keep refining the fit at your fittings until it's right. For a ready-to-wear order, text or email us and we'll help.
