How a commission works

A feeling becomes a masterpiece

This is how the world's best-dressed work with their designers. You tell her the occasion and how you want to feel, then you trust her eye with the rest. No screenshots, no copies.

  1. Design consultation · 30 min

    The feeling

    Brief your designer: the occasion, the mood, the story. Romantic, regal, bold, ethereal. A word, a colour or a memory is enough.

  2. Within a week

    The design

    A design fee reserves Shelena's time. She designs your original piece and presents it as a hand-drawn illustration, with fabrics and an exact quote.

  3. Your decision

    The making

    Love it? A 50% deposit begins production in the Shelena Made It atelier, and your design fee counts toward the total. Not for you? You're free to stop there.

  4. The big day

    Your moment

    You wear something that has never existed before. Collected in person or shipped anywhere in the world.

The original promise

Every Shelena Made It piece is an original design. Shelena doesn't recreate dresses from other designers, retailers or photos. Your piece should be as one-of-a-kind as you are, and every designer's work deserves the same respect. Designs remain Shelena's until they're commissioned.

Most bespoke pieces take 2–4 months. Event coming up sooner? Rush service is 1–2 months for $200.

All questions →
Can you recreate a dress I saw online?

No, and that's the point. Shelena only creates original designs. Tell her what you love about it (the feeling, the colour, the drama) and she'll design something that's entirely yours.

How far ahead should I book?

Bespoke pieces usually take 2–4 months, so book as soon as you have a date. For weddings, 6 months or more gives you the most room for fittings.

My event is soon. Can you still help?

Often, yes. Rush service takes 1–2 months for an extra $200. Tell us your date when you book and we'll say honestly what's possible.

What happens at the consultation?

A relaxed 30-minute design conversation about your occasion, the feeling you want and your budget. No need to bring photos of other dresses: words, colours and memories are perfect. Shelena then designs and illustrates your original piece.

Do you work with clients outside Texas?

Yes. Design consultations happen by video, measurements are guided remotely, fittings can happen by mail, and finished pieces ship worldwide. See how remote commissions work.
